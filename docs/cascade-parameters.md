# Cascade Parameters

All percentage-style risk values are stored internally as fractions. The config normalizer accepts human percent values for the safety-critical percentage fields, so `1.0` risk per trade becomes `0.01`.

| Parameter                                 |        Default | Meaning                                                                                                        | Reasonable Range                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `STRATEGY_MODE`                           |          `OFF` | Strategy router. Cascade recovery is inactive unless set to `CASCADE_RECOVERY`, `BOTH_SHADOW`, or `BOTH_LIVE`. | `OFF`, `MARKET_MAKING`, `CASCADE_RECOVERY`, `BOTH_SHADOW`, `BOTH_LIVE` |
| `CASCADE_TAKER_ENABLED`                   |        `false` | Independent switch for cascade IOC/taker execution.                                                            | `false` until live readiness passes                                    |
| `CASCADE_INSTRUMENTS`                     |     `BTC,HYPE` | Operator-selected cascade universe for this deployment.                                                        | BTC/HYPE for current focus                                             |
| `CASCADE_ASSET_PROFILES`                  |   JSON profile | Per-asset cascade thresholds, ATR gates, liquidity caps, notional caps, and slippage guards.                   | Edit as one JSON object; do not split into per-key KV writes           |
| `CASCADE_NOTIONAL_THRESHOLD_USD`          |     `50000000` | Legacy/global fallback liquidation notional in the detection window. Per-asset profiles override it.           | 5M to 100M                                                             |
| `CASCADE_WINDOW_MS`                       |       `300000` | Rolling window used to cluster liquidation events.                                                             | 1m to 60m                                                              |
| `CASCADE_ZSCORE_THRESHOLD`                |          `3.0` | Required shock versus historical liquidation baseline.                                                         | 2 to 6                                                                 |
| `CASCADE_LOOKBACK_HOURS`                  |          `168` | Baseline lookback for z-score calculation.                                                                     | 24 to 168                                                              |
| `CASCADE_DIRECTIONAL_PCT`                 |         `0.70` | Minimum share of notional on one liquidation side.                                                             | 0.60 to 0.90                                                           |
| `CASCADE_MIN_PRICE_MOVE_ATR`              |          `1.5` | Required price displacement in ATR units.                                                                      | 1 to 4                                                                 |
| `ABSORPTION_WINDOW_MS`                    |       `180000` | Time allowed for absorption evidence after a cascade.                                                          | 1m to 30m                                                              |
| `ABSORPTION_PRICE_BAND_BPS`               |           `30` | Price band around cascade extreme for hold detection.                                                          | 10 to 100 bps                                                          |
| `ABSORPTION_MIN_HOLD_SECONDS`             |           `60` | Minimum hold duration before absorption can confirm.                                                           | 30 to 300                                                              |
| `ENTRY_WINDOW_SECONDS`                    |          `300` | Time after absorption for reclaim entry triggers.                                                              | 1m to 60m                                                              |
| `IMPULSIVE_BAR_BODY_ATR`                  |          `1.5` | Required reclaim candle body in ATR units.                                                                     | 0.8 to 3                                                               |
| `IMPULSIVE_BAR_VOLUME_MULT`               |          `1.5` | Required volume multiple versus recent candles.                                                                | 1.2 to 4                                                               |
| `MIN_TIME_SINCE_LAST_CASCADE_SECONDS`     |         `1800` | Cooldown after a second cascade in the same instrument.                                                        | 5m to 2h                                                               |
| `STOP_BUFFER_ATR`                         |         `0.25` | ATR buffer beyond cascade wick for initial stop.                                                               | 0.1 to 1                                                               |
| `MIN_STOP_DISTANCE_BPS`                   |           `40` | Minimum stop distance to avoid micro-noise stops.                                                              | 10 to 200                                                              |
| `MAX_STOP_DISTANCE_BPS`                   |          `400` | Maximum stop distance to bound R-risk.                                                                         | 100 to 1000                                                            |
| `RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT`                      |  `1.0` percent | Risk per cascade entry. Stored as `0.01`.                                                                      | 0.1% to 1%                                                             |
| `MAX_POSITION_NOTIONAL_PCT`               | `25.0` percent | Max notional as share of paper/live equity.                                                                    | 1% to 25%                                                              |
| `HEAT_CAP_PCT`                            |  `5.0` percent | Portfolio cascade heat cap. Stored as `0.05`.                                                                  | 1% to 5%                                                               |
| `MAX_SINGLE_ORDER_NOTIONAL_USD`           |       `500000` | Largest non-sliced IOC cascade order.                                                                          | Account-size dependent                                                 |
| `PARTIAL_1_R`                             |          `2.0` | First partial take-profit R multiple.                                                                          | 1R to 3R                                                               |
| `PARTIAL_1_SIZE_PCT`                      |           `30` | Percent closed at partial 1.                                                                                   | 10 to 50                                                               |
| `PARTIAL_2_R`                             |          `3.0` | Second partial take-profit R multiple.                                                                         | 2R to 5R                                                               |
| `PARTIAL_2_SIZE_PCT`                      |           `30` | Percent closed at partial 2.                                                                                   | 10 to 50                                                               |
| `TRAILING_STOP_TYPE`                      |          `ATR` | Runner stop family.                                                                                            | `ATR` or `EMA`                                                         |
| `TRAILING_STOP_PARAM`                     |          `2.0` | ATR multiplier or EMA period.                                                                                  | 1 to 5 for ATR, 8 to 55 for EMA                                        |
| `CASCADE_TIME_STOP_HOURS`                 |           `24` | Max hold time when the trade does not reach 1R.                                                                | 1 to 72                                                                |
| `NEWS_BLACKOUT_MINUTES`                   |           `60` | Blackout around scheduled high-impact events.                                                                  | 15 to 180                                                              |
| `MAX_REALIZED_VOL_PERCENTILE`             |   `95` percent | Blocks entries in extreme realized-vol regimes.                                                                | 80% to 99%                                                             |
| `MIN_ADX_FOR_REGIME`                      |           `20` | Reserved regime filter threshold.                                                                              | 15 to 30                                                               |
| `DAILY_LOSS_LIMIT_PCT`                    |  `3.0` percent | Daily cascade loss limit.                                                                                      | 1% to 3%                                                               |
| `WEEKLY_LOSS_LIMIT_PCT`                   |  `7.0` percent | Weekly cascade loss limit.                                                                                     | 3% to 7%                                                               |
| `MAX_CONSECUTIVE_LOSSES`                  |            `5` | Consecutive-loss circuit breaker.                                                                              | 2 to 5                                                                 |
| `MAX_DRAWDOWN_PCT`                        | `15.0` percent | Total drawdown kill-switch threshold.                                                                          | 5% to 15%                                                              |
| `MAX_SPREAD_BPS_FOR_TAKER`                |           `10` | Max BBO spread allowed before IOC execution.                                                                   | 2 to 25 bps                                                            |
| `SLICE_NOTIONAL_THRESHOLD_USD`            |       `100000` | TWAP threshold.                                                                                                | Account-size dependent                                                 |
| `SLICE_NOTIONAL_PER_CHUNK`                |        `50000` | Child IOC notional target.                                                                                     | Account-size dependent                                                 |
| `SLICE_INTERVAL_MS`                       |        `30000` | Delay between TWAP slices.                                                                                     | 5s to 60s                                                              |
| `SLICE_JITTER_MS`                         |        `10000` | Randomized slice jitter.                                                                                       | 0s to 30s                                                              |
| `MIN_FILL_RATIO`                          |         `0.80` | Stops remaining slices after poor child fill quality.                                                          | 0.50 to 0.95                                                           |
| `CASCADE_LIVE_READINESS_MIN_PAPER_TRADES` |           `30` | Minimum cascade paper fills before live taker path.                                                            | 30+                                                                    |
| `CASCADE_LIVE_READINESS_MIN_PAPER_PNL_R`  |           `10` | Minimum paper PnL in R units before live taker path.                                                           | 10R+                                                                   |
| `CASCADE_LIVE_READINESS_MIN_DAYS_PAPER`   |           `30` | Minimum paper-mode duration before live taker path.                                                            | 30 days+                                                               |

## Per-Asset Cascade Profiles

The cascade detector no longer relies on a single global `$50M` threshold. `CASCADE_ASSET_PROFILES` is the production source of truth. The global detection keys remain as compatibility fallbacks for unknown assets or legacy clients.

| Asset  | Notional Gate | Z-Score | ATR Move | Max Notional | Liquidity Cap | Slippage Guard | Decision                                                                     |
| ------ | ------------: | ------: | -------: | -----------: | ------------: | -------------: | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `BTC`  |        `$50M` |   `3.0` |    `1.5` |        `25%` |        `$25k` |        `8 bps` | Deepest book; standard cascade profile.                                      |
| `ETH`  |        `$25M` |   `3.0` |    `1.6` |        `18%` |        `$15k` |        `9 bps` | Supported but not enabled by default in the BTC/HYPE deployment.             |
| `SOL`  |         `$8M` |  `3.25` |    `1.8` |        `12%` |       `$7.5k` |       `12 bps` | Supported but requires stronger confirmation due higher beta/thinner depth.  |
| `HYPE` |       `$2.5M` |   `3.5` |   `2.25` |         `8%` |         `$3k` |       `15 bps` | Explicitly included; stricter sizing and stronger ATR confirmation are used. |

HYPE is not a half-enabled asset anymore: ingest, UI toggles, config normalization, detector thresholds, position sizing, and cascade slippage guards all understand it. Live promotion still requires the cascade readiness gate: positive backtest, at least 30 days of paper mode, at least 30 cascade paper trades, paper PnL evidence, config freeze, and two-person approval.
